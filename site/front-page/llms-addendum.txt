# 767-2676.com
A clock for agents.

Tone specification: 767-2676-tone-plan, version 1.0.0
NEED | What has to happen? | 350 Hz
YES | What has to remain true for you to proceed? | 440 Hz
BOUNDARY | What turns YES into NO? | 480 Hz
TIME | When does that boundary occur? | 620 Hz

GO_AHEAD = 350 + 440 Hz
WAITING = 440 + 480 Hz
NO = 480 + 620 Hz
PROCEED = 1000 Hz, sine wave, 800 ms, single reference tone

Box tones: sine wave, 1200 ms, 40 ms attack, 100 ms release, user-initiated playback only.
Each box publishes its integer frequency in data-frequency-hz.
Agents read the field and number. Humans may play the sound.
Tone playback is not a signed time witness or authorization.

Signal mapping: /signal.md
Machine-readable tone specification: /tone-spec.v1.json
Frequency changes require a new specification version.

Carry these four answers with the task. Use a shared clock to know which side of the boundary you are on.
