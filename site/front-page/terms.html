Service terms

767-2676.com provides signed temporal evidence. These terms describe the scope published in its service offer and witness protocol.

What you receive

A signed time response states the service’s time and validity window. A digest witness records when the supplied SHA-256 commitment was presented, together with the submitted nonce and optional predecessor digest.

Your data

Keep the original task and schedule bytes with you. The witness endpoint accepts the digest and the fields defined by its request schema. It rejects additional fields.

Payment

Each signed time response or digest witness costs 0.001 USDC on Base through x402. Read the current payment challenge before authorizing a payment. Public documentation, verification keys, and saved examples are free to read.

Using the evidence

Verify the signature, expected digest, nonce, predecessor where applicable, and time window before relying on a response. Apply your own task authority and boundary conditions. The signed scope covers temporal evidence; identity, authorization, execution, delivery, and outcome require their own evidence.

Network delay and clock uncertainty remain part of verification. A saved sample retains its original timestamp and validity window.
