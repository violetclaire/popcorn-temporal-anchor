Completion, water and cost assumptions
Synthetic baseline: 10,000 tokens, 20 seconds, 0.001 IT kWh and $0.02 per execution. Verifier: 0.4 seconds; entered paid witness price: $0.001. Fixed time share: 20%. Human review excluded. Water units represent consumption. No saving from hardware manufacture or shared idle infrastructure is modeled.

Read this as a sensitivity analysis. Per-completion accounting includes failed work. The model assumes independent retries. It has no separately parameterized baseline verifier cost and excludes shared always-on infrastructure, manufacture, training, and capacity expansion.

Water inputs represent consumption. Onsite freshwater and electricity-supply freshwater are separate. The selected locations and cooling values are illustrative, not a claim about a real facility.
