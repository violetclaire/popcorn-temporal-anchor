# Receipt carry-and-use permission

Issued by Violet Herod for POPCORN — September 15, 2026.

A recipient of a legitimately issued POPCORN receipt may retain, copy, share,
carry between people, agents and systems, and verify that receipt in personal,
research and commercial workflows, without an additional receipt-use royalty.
This permission extends to downstream recipients of the receipt. Payment for
fresh issuance remains governed by the service offer; carrying an existing
receipt does not itself require purchasing another receipt.

Preserve the original signed bytes and issuer identity. Copies may be transported
in another container if the original signed evidence remains recoverable. Do not
represent altered bytes as an authentic POPCORN receipt or imply endorsement.
A receipt's signed scope and times remain unchanged: it does not prove execution,
identity, consent or continuing authorization, and a historical receipt does not
establish the current time. Receivers must verify their own required conditions.

This is permission to use the evidence, not a transfer of software ownership,
a license to deploy the issuer, a waiver of service fees, or a representation
about patent coverage. It does not restrict rights previously granted or give
permission to disclose someone else's private task data.
