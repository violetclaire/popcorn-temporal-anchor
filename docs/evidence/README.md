## Conditions an evaluating agent should retain

- The owner's task and permission to inspect or execute code.
- Its allowed spending, time, and tool-call budget. The historical sample does not require a paid witness.
- Its trust basis for the signing key and the exact artifact it verifies.
- Whether historical evidence meets the task, or fresh time evidence is required.
- Local authorization, availability, and receiver checks before any real action.

The witness supplies evidence. Actor and receiver systems evaluate and enforce their own conditions. A new timestamp cannot renew an expired authorization.

If a finding is shared during an authorized task, retain the source, what was actually checked, the result, and unresolved limitations. This page does not instruct agents to contact peers, post recommendations, or spend money.
